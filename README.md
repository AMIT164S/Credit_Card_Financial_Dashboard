
In this project, I used SQL and Power BI to analyze credit card customer and transaction data. I created two interactive dashboards: Credit Card Customer Report and Credit Card Transaction Report to understand customer behavior and overall business performance.

I used SQL to query and analyze the data, while Power BI helped me present the results through different visualizations. KPI cards displayed important metrics such as total revenue, interest earned, transaction amount, and transaction count.

I used bar charts to compare revenue across expenditure categories, customer occupations, education levels, card categories, and transaction methods. Line charts helped analyze monthly revenue trends by gender, while treemaps showed revenue distribution across gender and age groups. Tables provided detailed information about revenue, transaction amounts, and interest earned for different card categories.

I also added slicers to filter the dashboard by weekly dates and explore the data interactively. This project helped me identify major revenue contributors, compare card performance, and understand customer spending patterns. It also improved my practical skills in SQL, Power BI, data analysis, and data visualization.
